2. Your manager will not think about your career, they are there to extract work out of you. They will be polite and supportive as long as they want something from you and once they are done, they will ignore you and you are not needed anymore. This is also true other way around, sub-ordinates will be around for their needs and wants *don't get attached to anyone*.
3. Disclose information on need to know basis only. You give information strategically to win trust and make friends but these thing depend.
4. Don't speak too much at office about personal finances or family life learn from Americans. They could be leaking this information to your bosses. Plant information which will knowingly like a cannery to weed out these snitches. Keep you answer sweet and short.
